Client Platform Preview

A first look at the client platform we are building for Humble Services.

These are pictures of our test version. The platform is not on this website yet, so there is nothing on this page to fill in. The names, fees, call length, open times and time zones in the pictures are samples we made up, not prices or plans; yours will replace them.

On a phone, tap "Open the full-size picture" under any picture to see it larger.

Booking a free conversation

An owner picks a day, then a time, and tells us a little about the business. The owner can choose which U.S. time zone the times are shown in, and only the hours you choose to offer appear.

Your weekly view

Your five weekly numbers, every lead on one board from first contact through to onboarding, the calls coming up, the follow-ups still to do, and the status of every invoice. Fulfillment hours are not tracked yet.

Proposal and signature

The owner reads the proposal, types their name and ticks a box to agree. We keep a record of who signed, when, and the exact version they signed. Your attorney should confirm this is enough for your engagement letters.

The owner's account

Signing prepares the owner's account. Until email is connected, the set-up link is passed to the owner by hand, and the owner finishes setting up the account by choosing a password. The owner is offered two-step sign-in, with a code from an app on their phone. Their account shows their work with you, their documents and invoices, in plain words, on a computer or a phone. It has a place for assessment results; entering them is not built yet.

Where each launch check stands

These are the nine checks from the Launch Gates column of your map. We will show you each one working before we call the platform ready.

  • A proposal can be sent and signedPartlySigning works in our test version. Until email is connected, the proposal link is passed on by hand.
  • Payment can be collected correctlyNot yetNo card payment service is connected yet, so payments are recorded by hand in the test version.
  • Onboarding happens automaticallyPartlySigning sets up the client's records and account invitation by itself. The welcome email is not sent yet, so for now the link is passed on by hand.
  • Deliverables can be created and deliveredWorks in our test versionA report is added and shared with the owner through a link that expires.
  • Revenue and expenses hit the internal dashboardPartlySigned contract values and invoice status show. Expenses wait for your choice of accounting program.
  • One complete test client journey works without manual patchingPartlyThe whole journey runs in our test version, but the email and payment steps are still done by hand.
