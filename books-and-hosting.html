Bookkeeping: each person works inside the platform

Your CPA, CFO and accountants each get their own login and do their work in the platform itself. Downloads stay available for back-up. None of this is built yet: this is the design we propose. Here is how we suggest the work splits; tell us if your team divides it differently.

CFO

Sees all of the books and every report. Signs off each month once it is complete.

Your CPA

Sees the books, makes year-end and tax adjustments, and locks a month once it is closed.

Accountants

The day-to-day work: record entries, match payments to invoices, and send invoices.

  • Every change carries the name of the person who made it, and when.
  • Nothing is erased. A mistake is fixed with a correcting entry, so the history stays whole.
  • A locked month cannot be changed.

Why the Mac mini

We recommend your own Mac mini over renting cloud hosting because:

  • Your clients' financial records are kept on a machine you own, not on a cloud hosting company's servers. The exceptions are email, which stays with Bluehost, and, later, a second locked back-up copy kept somewhere else, whose key only you hold. It also means the machine and that key are in your care: if the key is lost, the locked copies cannot be opened, even by us. We help you store it safely when we set this up.
  • The AI helper runs on the mini itself, so client data is designed not to go to an AI company.

Can one mini handle thousands of clients?

We tested the database on a stand-in computer, not the mini, with 5,000 made-up businesses. Each took up very little room. Three screens got slow at that size; we have tested a fix on the stand-in computer that made them much faster (a faster way for the platform to check who may see each record), and it goes in before launch. The mini itself gets the same test at step 6, and we show you the result before anything goes live.
