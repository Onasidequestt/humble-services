What this work is, and what it is not

We offer practical business advisory and decision support. That includes the human side of ownership: workload, delegation, conflict, difficult employees or customers, and what you want the business to become.

Owner advisory is not psychotherapy or mental-health treatment. We are not a law firm, an accounting or tax firm, a business broker, a valuation firm or an investment adviser. When a question needs one of those professionals, we say so and help you prepare for that conversation.†

What we believe

Placeholder — Clark to supply: confirm, rewrite or remove these draft principles (written from the business plan and the website brief, not from the founder)

  • Owner-led businesses matter. Companies with a handful of people up to about forty are where owners, employees, customers and families meet most directly. When one of these businesses is healthy, it supports many lives. When it depends entirely on one person, all of that is at risk.
  • Transition planning starts years early. Clean financials, documented systems, a capable team and a spread of customers take time to build. Starting early gives an owner choices, instead of letting the market decide the value for them.
  • More revenue is not always a better business. A bigger company can be a harder one to own. The goal is a business that is financially healthy, operationally sound, personally sustainable for the owner, and able to succeed without permanent dependence on that owner.
  • A good business should work financially, operationally and personally.
  • Straight answers beat jargon.
  • Owners deserve an outside view before a big decision, not after.
  • Recommend more work only when it is genuinely warranted.
