Business Health Assessment

A broad diagnostic of the company's financial, operational, staffing, owner, risk and strategic health.

What you get: A prioritized picture of what is working, what is not, what needs immediate attention, and what deserves a closer look.

Comprehensive Business Diagnostic

A deeper review with document analysis, owner interviews and operational review.

What you get: Prioritized findings and a practical 90-day action plan.

Preliminary working range — to be confirmed$3,500–$6,500

Exit & Succession Readiness

A structured assessment and multi-year roadmap for a sale, family transition, management buyout or other succession path, with optional advisory support while you work through the roadmap.

Owner Advisory & Decision Support

Ongoing, confidential† strategy conversations about decisions, priorities, conflict, workload, delegation, employees, customers and the future you want. This is business advisory, not psychotherapy.

What you get: A sounding board for the decisions that come with owning a business.

Preliminary working range — to be confirmed$750–$1,250 per month

Strategic Business Advisory

A more active recurring relationship with analysis, implementation support, accountability and a monthly operating review.

What you get: A steady outside advisor who knows the business.

Preliminary working range — to be confirmed$1,500–$3,000 per month

What these services are not

We do not give legal, tax, securities or investment advice, do not prepare formal valuations, and do not provide psychotherapy or mental-health treatment. No engagement promises a sale price, a valuation or a financial result. When you need a CPA, attorney, valuation professional or broker, we will tell you and help you prepare.†
