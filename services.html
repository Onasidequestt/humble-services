Advise

Ongoing support with decisions over time. Exit & Succession Readiness also offers implementation advisory; it is listed under Assess, with its assessment.

Both advisory services follow the same shape. What differs between them is how active the work is and how often we meet.

  • Cadence. A standing monthly meeting, held on the same schedule each month, plus email or message access between meetings for anything that cannot wait.
  • What's covered. Whatever is live for the business that month — a decision the owner is weighing, a problem with an employee, a pricing question, a system that isn't working, or checking progress on something from an earlier assessment or project.
  • How it follows the assessment. Advisory work is not a stand-alone service. It picks up from a Vantage Point Review or a prior project: the open items and priorities identified there are the starting agenda, and each month's conversation is measured against them.
  • Strategic Business Advisory adds a monthly operating review — a short written look at how the business moved against its priorities that month — plus more active support implementing agreed changes, not just discussing them.
  • What it is not. Advisory work is a sounding board and an outside set of eyes, not therapy, not legal or financial advice, and not a promise of any particular result.

What these services are not

We do not give legal, tax, securities or investment advice, do not prepare formal valuations, and do not provide psychotherapy or mental-health treatment. No engagement promises a sale price, a valuation or a financial result. When you need a CPA, attorney, valuation professional or broker, we will tell you and help you prepare.
