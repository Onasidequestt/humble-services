An independent, comprehensive multi-angle assessment of your business.

Seeing the whole business with fresh eyes. Understanding what connects it and what the foundations are behind it. Taking that information and leading the way to know what and how to improve it.

The six areas

Financial Performance

Revenue quality, margins, pricing, cash flow and the reliability of the numbers.

Operations & Systems

How work gets done, what is written down, and where it gets stuck.

People & Organization

Roles, responsibilities, key people and how decisions move.

Owner Role & Sustainability

Your workload, what only you can do, and whether the role still fits the life you want.

Risk & Resilience

Customer concentration, key-person risk and what could knock the business off course.

Transferability & Exit Readiness

Whether the company could be owned and run well by someone else.

What you receive

  • A scorecard across the six areas.
  • A map of how the business actually works — how the parts connect and where the money moves through it.
  • A summary of the main strengths and weak spots.
  • Priorities sorted into Fix Now, Improve Next and Build Toward.
  • A before-and-after snapshot, and levers to see what a change could mean before you make it.
  • A monthly trend, so progress against the plan is visible over time.
  • A 90-day action plan for the first priorities.
  • A recommendation for further work only when it is genuinely warranted.

The free Scorecard reflects your own answers. The paid Humble Services Vantage Point Review is the founder reviewing your real numbers, documents and team, with a 90-day plan.

How it is designed to run

  1. A free conversation to confirm the assessment fits.
  2. You complete a questionnaire and share the documents on a short checklist.
  3. An assessment meeting to walk through the findings.
  4. You receive the scorecard, priorities and 90-day plan.

The plan is to request detailed financial records only once a secure way to share them is in place.
