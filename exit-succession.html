Exit & Succession Readiness

The best time to prepare a business for sale is years before it is listed.

We help owners identify what makes a company transferable, and what must change before the market decides the value for them. That applies whether the next owner is a buyer, a family member, your management team or someone not yet in view.

This may be for you if

  • You would like to step back or sell in the next three to five years, but there is no plan yet.
  • A family member, employee or manager may take over, but nothing is structured.
  • The business is profitable, yet most of it still runs through you.
  • You are not sure what a buyer or successor would see.

What we look at

Owner dependence

What stops or slows when you step away.

Management depth

Who could make decisions, and whether they are ready.

Customer concentration

How much rests on a few accounts or relationships.

Documented systems

Whether the way work gets done is written down.

Financial credibility

Whether the numbers are clear and consistent enough to be trusted by someone new.

Recurring revenue

How predictable the income is from year to year.
